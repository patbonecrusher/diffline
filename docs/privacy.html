Privacy Policy

Diffline does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

  • The app has no accounts, analytics, telemetry, or advertising, and makes no network connections.
  • Files and folders you compare are read locally and never leave your Mac. The app runs inside the macOS App Sandbox and only accesses items you open, drop, or save.
  • The Recent list stores references to items you compared, in the app's own container on your Mac. Clear it at any time from the start window.
  • Preferences (such as font size and comparison options) are stored locally in the app's own container.

Because nothing is collected, there is nothing to share with third parties and nothing to request or delete.

Questions: open an issue on GitHub.
