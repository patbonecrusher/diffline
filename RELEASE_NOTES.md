Diffline 1.0.2

- **Change overview.** A strip beside the scroll bar shows every change in the file at a glance: red for removed, green for added, blue for modified, with a box around the part on screen. Click or drag in it to jump. In a three-way merge, open conflicts are orange. Toggle it with View › Show Change Overview (⌥⌘M) or in Settings.

Homebrew: `brew upgrade --cask diffline`
